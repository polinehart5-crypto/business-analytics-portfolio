# business-analytics-portfolio
A collection of business analytics projects applying Python, R, and UML to analyse financial data, visualise trends and produce actionable insights.

## Projects

- [British Airways Reviews Analysis](BA_reviews_analysis/)  
  *Python | EDA, text mining, customer satisfaction trends (2015–2023)*  
  → Identified critical drivers of loyalty and highlighted operational issues.  

- [Customer Segmentation & Association Rules](customer_segmentation_analysis/)  
  *Python | K-means clustering, Apriori rules, financial services use case*  
  → Built customer segments and actionable rules for targeted product offerings.  

- [Bitcoin Market Behavior Analysis](bitcoin_market_analysis/)  
  *R | Time-series, hypothesis testing, regression*  
  → Analyzed Bitcoin’s volatility, returns, and trading activity (2014–2021).
